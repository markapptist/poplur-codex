8. Improvement signals the evidence supports

The closest things to "how the app could be improved" that exist. Each comes from a machine or a developer, and says so.

The first message to anyone never sent fixed

The synthetic testers' direct-message step failed 110 times and never once succeeded. The cause was in the app, not the runner: the client read the message document before writing the batch, the participant lookup returned nothing for a thread that did not exist yet, and the rules denied it. This is the strongest product defect the Test Center has surfaced, and it surfaced as a step failure, not as feedback. Production now has its first thread.

Evidence: synthetic-runner.log "step send-dm failed" ×110, " messaged " ×0; iOS commit 8f28662b

Testers never reach the hold menu undetermined

The hold gesture failed 131 times in the synthetic journeys, 123 of them "did not settle on hold-menu (saw feed)"; it is the current error of one runner actor. Whether this is a runner selector problem or a real discoverability problem for a person holding the cube is not established by any evidence found. Worth a human test.

Evidence: synthetic-runner.log "step open-hold-menu failed" ×131, "saw feed" ×123

A newcomer with nothing nearby sees an empty app mitigated

Apple rejected the app as "not complete" because radius-gated delivery meant a reviewer outside every post's radius saw nothing. The 29 official posts reach the whole planet to fix that. The reviewer's own words are not stored in any repository; only a developer's paraphrase survives. The same emptiness awaits any first user in a quiet area.

Evidence: qa/official-launch-content/seed-official-posts.mjs header; 29 posts with officialContent: true

Silent front-end failures would be invisible to the desk open

The runner captures console errors and page errors in memory only (a 20-line tail) and the session documents carry no such fields. Across the whole log it recorded zero of either, which is either good news or a blind spot; today it cannot tell which.

Evidence: testSyntheticBrowserSessions schema; runner log pageerror ×0, console.error ×0

Infrastructure, not product info

Twenty connection refusals to the RN shell on 8081, one 75-second page timeout, and 32,230 sandbox "blocked" verdicts are the runner's environment and its safety net doing their jobs. The scheduler cannot restart a runner that is down by design, so once the cohort stops it stays stopped until someone starts it.

Evidence: synthetic-runner.log ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED ×20; live-production-blocked ×32,230; external-write-blocked ×1,381; live-firestore-blocked ×13

9. Proposed improvements — for review

  1. A "Send feedback" row in web Settings

    Why
    No human can tell the desk anything: no app has a feedback row, form or mail link, and the in-app browser blocks mailto:. The backend that would store it already exists and has never been called.
    Evidence
    feedback: In-app "Send feedback" received 0; testFeedback received 0; grep submitBetaFeedback → definition only (PoplurApp/src/services/testControl.ts:619)
    Change
    Add a Settings row in poplur-clay-map with the three fields the testFeedback backend already stores (finding, liked, improve), calling the existing beta-feedback action. The desk's "What testers are saying" section already renders those documents.
    Risk
    Low: a new write path to an existing callable whose rule already exists. Needs a rules-deploy check and a 64-character cap on free text.

  2. Prompt the "Share closed-beta activity" switch once after first login

    Why
    0 of 5 accounts have consented, so the human telemetry collection is empty and "Who is testing right now" has never had a row. The switch is opt-in and nobody has found it.
    Evidence
    users where testActivityConsent EQUAL true → 0; testActivity count → 0
    Change
    Show the switch once on the first post-login launch of a beta build, defaulting to off, and record the answer on users/{uid} so the prompt never repeats.
    Risk
    Medium: a consent prompt is a product surface whose wording needs review; a wrong default would collect without consent.

Nothing here changes anything until it is approved: these are proposals for review, derived from the evidence above, and none of them came from a user.
