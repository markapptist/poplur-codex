## Message 1 — the 15 Sep welcome post, edited in place (pinned)

**Welcome to the Poplur focus group**
You're the first real people to use Poplur. Everything before you was a synthetic test cohort — AI actors posting on the live backend — so what you do in October shapes the product, and goes into our YC application.

**What Poplur is**
A social map. Every post ("pop") lands on a 3D map where you made it, with a radius. Everyone inside that radius sees it — no followers needed. When people engage, the radius grows and the pop spreads; when they don't, it shrinks. Reach is earned by the post, not the account.

**The beta runs Thu Oct 1 – Fri Oct 31.** Join now so you're signed and installed before it opens. iPhone (XS or newer, iOS 18.5+) and Android (8.0 or newer).

**Getting started**
1. Sign the tester agreement at https://www.poplur.ca — enter your email, read it, sign. Two minutes. Short version: what you see here stays here, no screenshots outside this server, and we can use your feedback.
2. Your install links arrive by email as soon as you sign: TestFlight for iPhone, Google Play internal testing for Android. Android: DM me the Google account email on your phone first, so I can add you to the Play test list before the link works.
3. Install, allow location, and post your first pop from campus (or wherever you are). You can install and post before Oct 1; points start counting on Oct 1.

**This is optional and has nothing to do with grades.** Not a course, not an assignment, no effect on your standing anywhere. Leave whenever you like.

Questions → ask here. Thanks for doing this. — Mark
