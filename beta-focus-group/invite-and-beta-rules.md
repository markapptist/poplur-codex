## Message 2 — pinned in the server (#welcome or #focus-group), visible to everyone

**How the Poplur beta works (Oct 1 – Oct 31)**

Poplur is a social map: you post from where you are, your post gets a radius, everyone inside it sees it, and the radius grows when people engage. It's private — please don't screenshot or share it outside this server.

**Getting started**
1. Sign the NDA: [NDA link]. Put your Discord username on it — and if a friend referred you, their Discord username too. Android: also the Google account email on your phone, so I can add you to the Play test list.
2. I'll give you the **Tester** role — that unlocks #focus-group with the install links (TestFlight for iPhone, Google Play internal testing for Android).
3. iPhone: install TestFlight, then Poplur. Android: open the Play testing link signed in as the Google account on your NDA, accept, then install Poplur from the Play Store. Allow location. Post your first pop from campus (or wherever you are). You can install and post before Oct 1; points start counting on Oct 1.

**What you get out of it**
- **Resume line + reference** for everyone who files all four reports: "Beta tester, Poplur (iOS/Android social map), Oct 2026" — and a reference or LinkedIn recommendation from me on request.
- **Founding tester credit** for the top four by points at the end of Oct 31: your name in the app's credits and on poplur.ca, permanently. Ties all get it.
- **First access** to what ships next, and a demo night at the end for everyone who finished all four reports.
- **Report — 10 pts each.** Four reports: **Sun Oct 4**, **Sun Oct 11**, **Wed Oct 14** (closes the first two weeks), and a final one by **Sat Oct 31**. ~5 minutes each, on the site: https://www.poplur.ca/report (your own link is in your beta-links email). Late = 5 pts, as long as it's in before the next one is due.
- **Confirmed bug — 5 pts each.** Post it in #bugs with a screenshot; it counts when I mark it ✅. First person to report it gets the points.
- **Voice chat — 5 pts** for each week you show up.
- **Referral — 5 pts each**, up to 5 — someone who names you on their NDA and sends in their first report.
- Standings posted in #focus-group every Monday.
- Points come from testing work, not from how much you use the app — use it the way you naturally would.

**Voice chat** — Tuesdays 7–7:30 pm in 🔊 focus-group on **Oct 6, Oct 13, Oct 20 and Oct 27**. Talk through what you found; 5 pts for showing up, no penalty for skipping.

**Ask anything in #focus-group** — bugs, confusion, "why did my radius shrink", all of it is useful. The first two weeks (Oct 1–14) are the busy part with a daily prompt; after that it's one prompt a week and the board stays open. The points board closes Oct 31; you keep the app. If anything about the beta changes, I'll post it here with at least a week's notice.
